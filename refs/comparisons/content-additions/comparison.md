## What the user suspected: is a candidate already in the pack?

- Vanilla Backport and Bedrockoid generate fallen trees. Bedrockoid ships `data/bedrockoid/forge/biome_modifier/fallen_*_tree.json` for oak, birch, spruce, dark oak, jungle and the big spruce and jungle trees. Vanilla Backport ships `data/minecraft/worldgen/placed_feature/placed_fallen_*_tree.json` plus biome tags (`spawns_fallen_*_trees`) for oak, birch, super birch, spruce, jungle and poplar. They use different feature names, so both would place trees in the same forest (inference; not run). Vanilla Backport's `CommonConfig` contains the string `fallen`, so it probably has a switch (not read). No other item of Bedrockoid's feature list (snow under trees, leaves with snow, reach-around placement, dyed shulkers, and so on) was found in Vanilla Backport's lists of blocks, items, entities and data files; this was a name check, not an exhaustive one.
- Nothing else overlaps. No installed mod does spawn animations (the Fresh Animations zip has no file named after spawn, dig or emerge), 3D skin layers, zombie senses, or any of the engineering and magic systems. Create Crafts & Additions is the one engineering mod that needs an installed mod (Create).
- Engineering: the user's reading is right. Mekanism (machines and generators), Create Crafts & Additions (Create and electricity) and AE2 (storage and autocrafting) share only the energy unit (FE).

## Decisions only the user can make

1. Wither Storm: the evidence is against it. The two open reports match this pack's stack exactly (Oculus, Embeddium, Distant Horizons on 1.20.1 Forge, mod 4.2.1), and the author's answer is that proper Distant Horizons support is still to be written. It is also a very large boss that, by its own description, destroys everything it can, and its last 1.20.1 build is from 2024-08. Recommended: do not add. If wanted anyway, drop Distant Horizons for that world or accept the vanishing.
2. Ice and Fire: original or Community Edition.
   - Original: only a beta build exists for 1.20.1 Forge (2024-08-15). It needs Citadel. Open reports: a Distant Horizons crash (#5416) and invisible or transparent mobs with shaders.
   - Community Edition (Modrinth `iceandfire-ce`): a fork with 1.20.1 Forge builds up to 1.2.7 (2026-07-18), 2.2 million downloads. Its page says it drops the Myrmex series, adds netherite items, supports EMF and ETF (installed), and warns that saves from the original must not be opened with it. It needs Uranus (which needs Architectury 9.2.14 or newer) and Jupiter. No Oculus report and one closed Distant Horizons report were found in its tracker (a small user base).
   - Choose before the first world, because the two are not save-compatible.
3. Magic: Iron's Spells, Ars Nouveau and Botania. Iron's and Ars both add spellcasting with their own item sets; Botania is a separate mana and flower system. No conflict was found, so this is a scope choice. Iron's Spells uses playerAnimator for cast poses, and Punchy! Moves rewrites player poses; that pair is untested.
4. Touhou Little Maid: its open server crash (#1146) is on `PersistentEntitySectionManager.tick`, after long-distance `/tp`, and the mod's own hook sits on that path. Waystones teleports are long-distance too (inference). Acceptable on a single-player pack; on a shared server it is a known risk without a fix.
5. Zombie Awareness and TaCZ: gunshots are not "sounds" for Zombie Awareness. A small add-on, TACZ-Sound Attracts Zombies (Modrinth `tacz-sound-attracts-zombies`, updated 2026-07-30, about 24,000 downloads), bridges them. It is a separate mod that would need its own review.

## Risks that apply to all of them

- Scale: the pack has 91 mod metafiles. The 19 candidates plus Almost Unified are 20 mods, and they need 8 libraries with the original Ice and Fire (Platform, CoroUtil, GuideME, GeckoLib, Iron's Lib, playerAnimator, Patchouli, Citadel) or 10 with the Community Edition (Uranus, Architectury and Jupiter instead of Citadel).
- `refs/issues.md` records that nothing was measured and that most entries are unverified, including Create 6.0.8 with Colorwheel, Embeddium, Flerovium and Oculus. Adding 20 mods at once makes a crash hard to trace. Add in groups and launch after each (suggested order: libraries and engineering, then magic, then world and End content).
- Distant Horizons: its wiki says world generator mods (Biomes O' Plenty, Twilight Forest, Terralith and others) are supported and advises setting the generator mode to `surface` if a structure breaks under multithreaded generation. New world generation here: Vanilla Backport (three biomes, fallen trees), Deeper and Darker (a dimension), End Remastered (structures), Ice and Fire, Botania and Ars Nouveau (structures, flowers). A Discord answer in the Distant Horizons server names Fire and Ice (probably Ice and Fire) as a mod that dislikes multithreaded generation (unverified); the open Ice and Fire issue #5416 is the same kind of crash.
- FastSuite: `refs/issues.md` records older thread-safety crashes with Mekanism and AE2. Installed 5.1.2 parallelizes only vanilla recipe classes, so the risk is low. Check with spark after adding.
- Dormant 1.20.1 builds (no 1.20.1 release since 2024): Zombie Awareness, End Remastered, True Ending, Wither Storm, Ice and Fire (original), Deeper and Darker, Almost Unify Everything. They should still load on Forge 47.4.28, whose range they accept.
- Data packs: Spawn Animations and True Ending are listed as data packs. A data pack in a world folder is not distributed by packwiz. Both publish a Forge "mod" build for 1.20.1 (Spawn Animations `1.11.6+mod`, True Ending `1.1.0c+mod`, which uses the `lowcodefml` loader), so no data-pack loader mod is needed.
- Curios: Iron's Spells needs 5.14.1 or newer. The installed one is exactly 5.14.1.
